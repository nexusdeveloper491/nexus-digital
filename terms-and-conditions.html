02

Services, Deliverables & Timelines

Nexus Digital provides professional digital engineering and creative services, including but not limited to:

  • Custom Web Design & Frontend Development: Hand-crafted HTML5, CSS3, JavaScript, Vue/React architectures, responsive mobile-first layouts, and clean animations.
  • E-Commerce & Service Portals: Custom storefronts, catalog filters, booking workflows, and third-party checkout integrations.

Project delivery schedules provided in estimates represent good-faith operational forecasts. Timelines remain contingent upon the prompt provision of client content, branding assets, API credentials, and timely milestone approvals.

03

Quotations, Milestones & Payment Terms

All pricing quotations issued by Nexus Digital are valid for 30 calendar days from the date of issuance.

Standard Payment Milestone Structure

  • Initial Deposit (50%): Required prior to project initiation, wireframing, and code repository setup.
  • Staging Completion / Beta Review (25%): Invoiced upon demonstration of functional staging pages and core features.
  • Final Settlement (25%): Due upon final quality assurance review, prior to live DNS pointing, domain deployment, or unencrypted source code delivery.

Payment Methods

We accept direct Bank NEFT/IMPS transfers, UPI, International Wire, and Fiverr Escrow orders.

Payment Due Dates

Invoices are payable within 7 business days of issuance to avoid development pauses.

Late payments exceeding 14 calendar days may result in a temporary freeze of development staging environments until the outstanding milestone balance is cleared.

Revisions, Edits & Change Requests

Every standard custom web design proposal includes two (2) comprehensive rounds of revisions at both the wireframe/design preview stage and the beta staging stage.

Revisions include aesthetic adjustments, typography modifications, layout alignments, and copy alterations within the agreed Statement of Work.

Out-of-Scope Modifications: Requests for new architectural features, additional pages, database refactoring, or fundamental scope pivots not included in the original agreement will be estimated separately under a transparent Change Order at our standard hourly or fixed feature rate.

Complimentary 30-Day Post-Launch Warranty

We stand firmly behind the quality and reliability of our code. Nexus Digital provides a complimentary 30-Day Technical Warranty starting immediately from the official deployment/launch date.

Warranty Coverage Includes:

  • Fixing unexpected styling bugs or responsive rendering errors on supported modern browsers (Chrome, Firefox, Safari, Edge).
  • Correcting broken links, JavaScript errors, or faulty contact form dispatches built by our team.
  • Resolving SSL certificate binding or Vercel edge deployment issues directly related to the delivered code.

Exclusions: The warranty does not cover issues caused by unauthorized third-party modifications to the code, external API outages, server hardware failures, or major browser breaking engine updates released subsequent to launch.
