Agency FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our white-label partnership contracts and operating procedures.

1. Will our clients ever know Nexus Digital built their website?
Never. We operate 100% invisibly in the background. There will be zero Nexus Digital branding in the code, footer, commits, or metadata. Staging previews are hosted on unbranded neutral servers, and we sign a legally binding Non-Disclosure Agreement (NDA) with your agency.
2. How do custom partnership contracts work?
Because every agency has unique project scopes and client pipelines, we don't force you into rigid public tiers. We craft custom contracts based on your expected monthly volume (Per-Project, Dedicated Developer Retainers, or Maintenance SLAs) ensuring you maximize your agency profit margins while enjoying guaranteed delivery dates.
3. Can your developers work directly inside our agency's Slack or ClickUp?
Yes, absolutely. For dedicated developer retainers and active agency partners, our team can join your internal Slack, ClickUp, Asana, Trello, or Jira workspace using your agency domain email address (e.g. dev@youragency.com) to attend daily standups and provide real-time updates.
4. What happens if there is a bug or issue after client launch?
Every custom project contract includes our 30-Day Post-Launch Bug Warranty. If any code issue or responsive display bug arises after launch, our team fixes it immediately at zero additional charge. We also offer ongoing white-label maintenance retainers for continuous long-term support.
5. How do revisions and feedback cycles work?
We provide a dedicated review sprint on our private staging server. You or your project managers can test the build, drop visual feedback pins or notes, and our engineers implement revisions promptly until the site matches your exact approved design specifications.
6. How quickly can we start our first white-label project?
We can execute our NDA and kick off your first project or developer sprint within 24 to 48 hours of brief review. Simply click any consultation button or submit the partnership form below to begin.
